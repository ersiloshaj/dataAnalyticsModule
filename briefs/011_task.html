Subject: Capital Allocation Decision-Support Dashboard - Energy Transition Risk & Profitability

Welcome to Helios Energy Capital.

Our firm manages a diversified portfolio of energy assets across fossil fuels and renewable generation. Historically, fossil fuel plants have delivered stable returns. However, recent market conditions - including fuel price volatility, regulatory pressure, and technological improvements in renewables - are beginning to challenge that assumption.

You will work with energy system data derived from sources such as the Public Utility Data Liberation (PUDL) project, which integrates operational, financial, and fuel-level data across the electricity system.

Strategic Context

Senior leadership is currently split across two competing investment philosophies:

  • Yield preservation: Continue investing in fossil fuel assets delivering strong short-term returns
  • Transition positioning: Reallocate capital toward renewables in anticipation of structural market change

There is no consensus. Your analysis will directly inform this decision.

Your Role

You will operate as a Junior Investment Analyst. Your responsibility is to develop a dashboard that enables senior stakeholders to evaluate trade-offs between profitability, risk, and long-term viability.

Core Analytical Objectives

  • Cost efficiency: Compare generation cost per MWh across fuel types
  • Operational performance: Identify underperforming plants relative to peers
  • Volatility: Assess sensitivity to fuel price fluctuations
  • Transition risk: Identify assets exposed to regulatory or structural decline

Required KPIs

  • Cost per MWh (primary metric)
  • Capacity factor / utilisation rate
  • Fuel cost trends
  • Emissions intensity (proxy for regulatory exposure)
  • Output variability
Important: You are expected to design your own derived metrics. Where assumptions are made (e.g., normalisation across plants or fuel types), they must be explicitly documented.

Constraints

  • No complex black-box modelling
  • Data completeness will vary across assets
  • Comparability across fuel types must be handled carefully
  • Outputs must be usable by non-technical stakeholders

Expected Deliverables

  • Interactive RShiny dashboard with filtering by fuel type, region, plant, and time
  • Technical note (1–2 pages) describing data transformations and assumptions
  • Final recommendation: Maintain, rebalance, or transition portfolio

Success Criteria

We are not evaluating technical complexity alone. We are looking for:

  • Clear reasoning and structured thinking
  • Thoughtful KPI design
  • Ability to communicate trade-offs under uncertainty
  • A dashboard that genuinely supports decision-making
