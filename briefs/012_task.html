Strategic Note
Subject: Market Entry Support Dashboard - Growth, Risk & Opportunity

Welcome to Orion Global Holdings.

Our organisation is entering a new phase of international expansion. Previous expansion cycles have delivered mixed results, with several markets underperforming expectations due to overestimated demand and underestimated risk.

You will work with data from the World Bank World Development Indicators, which provide internationally comparable economic and demographic data across over 200 countries and thousands of indicators.

Orion Global Holdings is a diversified international business group with subsidiaries in consumer goods, retail, and digital commerce. It is evaluating new countries for expansion based on economic growth, population trends, internet access, and macroeconomic stability.

Strategic Context

The Executive Committee must select 3–5 new markets for expansion within the next 12 months.

  • High-growth markets offer strong upside but carry economic instability
  • Stable markets provide predictability but limited expansion potential

Whilst there is no “correct” answer - we expect a report which gives a balaced view of the economic market with honest and validated suggestions.

Your Role

You will operate as a Strategy Analyst. Your task is to build a dashboard that allows senior leadership to evaluate markets across growth, affordability, and risk dimensions.

Core Analytical Objectives

  • Growth potential: Identify economies with sustained GDP expansion
  • Consumer opportunity: Evaluate income levels and population growth
  • Infrastructure readiness: Assess digital and economic infrastructure
  • Macroeconomic risk: Identify instability (inflation, unemployment)

Required KPIs

  • GDP growth rate
  • GDP per capita
  • Population growth
  • Internet penetration
  • Inflation rate
  • Unemployment rate
Important: You must construct a Market Attractiveness Index. Weighting decisions must be justified and tested for sensitivity.

Constraints

  • Data availability varies significantly by country
  • No single KPI should dominate decision-making
  • Stakeholders require a shortlist of no more than 5 countries
  • Outputs must be interpretable by non-technical executives

Expected Deliverables

  • Interactive RShiny dashboard with global map and country comparison tools
  • Technical note explaining methodology and index construction
  • Final recommendation: ranked shortlist of expansion markets

Success Criteria

  • Clear and defensible reasoning
  • Balanced treatment of growth vs risk
  • Transparent methodology
  • Decision-oriented insights (not descriptive analysis)
