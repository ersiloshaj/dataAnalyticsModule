Policy Brief
Subject: Graduate Outcomes Dashboard - Institutional Performance, Subject Value & Inequality

Welcome to the Department for Education.

The UK higher education system is under increasing scrutiny to demonstrate both economic value and fair access. Recent analysis using Longitudinal Education Outcomes (LEO) data shows substantial variation in graduate outcomes across subjects, institutions, and demographic groups.

You will work with two complementary datasets derived from LEO:

  • Graduate Outcomes Dataset (Subject & Demographic Level); Provides earnings and employment outcomes by subject and graduate characteristics.
  • Provider Level Dataset (Institution Level); enables comparison of outcomes across universities and subjects.

Strategic Context

Government must balance competing priorities:

  • Access & participation: widening access to higher education
  • Economic value: ensuring degrees deliver strong labour market returns

Policy decisions increasingly rely on evidence of “value for money” across institutions and subjects.

Your Role

You will operate as a Data Analyst within the Skills & Outcomes Unit. Your task is to develop a dashboard that enables policymakers to evaluate where value is created, where it is uneven, and where intervention may be required.

Core Analytical Objectives

  • Institutional performance: Compare outcomes across universities
  • Subject value: Identify high- and low-return degree subjects
  • Outcome variation: Analyse differences within subjects across institutions
  • Equity analysis: Identify disparities across demographic groups

Required KPIs

  • Median earnings (1, 3, 5 years after graduation)
  • Employment rate (sustained employment)
  • Further study rate
  • Earnings distribution (quartiles)
  • Earnings growth over time
Important: LEO data is observational. Differences in outcomes may reflect student intake, subject mix, or regional labour markets - not institutional performance alone.

Constraints

  • Data is aggregated and does not imply causality
  • Some values may be suppressed for small groups
  • Comparisons across providers must be interpreted carefully
  • Outputs must be accessible to non-technical stakeholders

Expected Deliverables

  • Interactive RShiny dashboard with filters for institution, subject, demographics, and time
  • Technical note explaining data transformations and limitations
  • Policy brief outlining key findings and recommendations

Success Criteria

  • Clear, structured analysis
  • Balanced interpretation of data limitations
  • Effective comparison across institutions and subjects
  • Insights that support real policy decisions
