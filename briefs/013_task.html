Subject: Patient Flow & Capacity Dashboard - Efficiency, Cost & Quality

Welcome to the NorthCare Health Network.

Our hospitals are currently experiencing sustained operational pressure. Patient demand has increased significantly, leading to longer wait times, capacity constraints, and rising costs.

You will work with hospital operations data including patient admissions, treatment pathways, resource utilisation, and outcomes. Such datasets are commonly used to improve efficiency and resource allocation across healthcare systems.

Strategic Context

Leadership is balancing competing priorities:

  • Reduce wait times and improve patient experience
  • Maintain clinical quality and avoid increased re-admissions

Improving one metric at the expense of another is not acceptable.

Your Role

You will operate as a Healthcare Data Analyst. Your dashboard will support hospital leadership in understanding patient flow, capacity constraints, and operational bottlenecks.

Core Analytical Objectives

  • Flow analysis: Identify bottlenecks in the patient journey
  • Capacity management: Evaluate bed utilisation and department load
  • Efficiency drivers: Understand factors affecting length of stay
  • Cost analysis: Identify high-cost patient pathways

Required KPIs

  • Average wait time
  • Length of stay
  • Bed occupancy rate
  • Readmission rate
  • Cost per patient
Important: Length of stay is a critical operational metric. Reducing LOS can improve efficiency but must not compromise patient outcomes.

Constraints

  • Reducing wait time must not increase readmission rates
  • Data may be incomplete or simulated
  • Ethical considerations must be acknowledged
  • Outputs must be interpretable for clinical staff

Expected Deliverables

  • Interactive RShiny dashboard (time, department, patient type)
  • Operational analysis report identifying bottlenecks
  • Recommendations for improving efficiency without reducing care quality

Success Criteria

  • Clear identification of bottlenecks
  • Balanced operational trade-offs
  • Actionable recommendations
  • Clarity for non-technical stakeholders
