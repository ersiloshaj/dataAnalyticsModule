Subject: Critical Performance Dashboard - Race Pace, Tire Life & Strategic Delta

Team, we are in a fight for every single point this season. As we push the limits of the MCL40, we need a robust analytics tool that goes beyond simple lap timing. We need to visualise the "silent killers" of our race result: tire degradation and pit-loss efficiency.

Your mission is to build a decision-support dashboard that helps Mission Control identify exactly when our rivals are vulnerable to an undercut.

Strategic Directive: All analysis must be objective and data-driven. Please ensure the dashboard handles our team-mate comparison logic under the "Papaya Rules" directive - which, as a reminder, means race each other hard, but if you code a bug that takes both drivers out of the visualisation, Zak won't be happy.

Analytical Objectives

  • Tire Degradation Curves: Model lap time "fall-off" across Soft, Medium, and Hard compounds.
  • Rival Benchmarking: Compare our rolling average pace against Mercedes, Red Bull and Ferrari.
  • Pit Window Analysis: Visualise the "gap to traffic" to ensure we release drivers into clean air.
  • Outlier Handling: Automatically filter laps affected by Safety Cars or VSCs to avoid skewed averages.
Suggested KPI: Degradation Gradient (sec/lap)
Suggested KPI: Fuel-Corrected Pace Delta
Suggested KPI: Pit Lane Time Loss vs. Fresh Tire Advantage
Suggested KPI: Consistency Score (Std Dev of Lap Times)

Data Access & Infrastructure

You are expected to utilise the following raw data sources and services for this build:

  • FastF1 Python Library: The official documentation for accessing raw telemetry, tire compounds, and micro-sector data.
  • Jolpica-F1 API: The community-standard, drop-in replacement for the deprecated Ergast API. Use this for historical race results, pit stop timing, and standings.
  • OpenF1: Open API for lap data, positions, team radio, stints, and weather.
  • F1 CSV Archives: Historical lap timing for trend analysis.

Expected Deliverables

  • Interactive Dashboard: RShiny or Python Dash app with filtering for Driver, Circuit, and Year.
  • Technical Note: Description of your normalisation logic (e.g., how you adjusted for fuel burn).
  • Race Debrief Memo: 3 actionable insights from a historical race of your choice.
