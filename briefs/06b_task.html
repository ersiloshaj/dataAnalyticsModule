Subject: Retail Operations and Performance Dashboard - Sales, Inventory and Demand

The group is commissioning an internal dashboard to support store leadership, commercial teams and regional managers with fast, consistent insight into retail performance. The dashboard will be used to monitor sales, inventory health, pricing pressure and demand patterns across stores, regions and product categories.

This project uses the Retail & Supply Chain Operations Data dataset, which includes time-stamped records for inventory levels, units sold, units ordered, demand forecasts, pricing, discounts, competitor pricing, weather, holidays and seasonality.

Analytical standard: This tool is designed for decision support. You should describe patterns and plausible operational explanations, but avoid claiming causality. Document assumptions clearly, including data cleaning choices, missingness handling, and any engineered metrics.

data available

  • Store, region and product category identifiers
  • Time-stamped sales and inventory information
  • Units sold, units ordered and demand forecast
  • Price, discount and competitor pricing
  • Weather, holiday/promotion and seasonality indicators

primary dashboard objectives

  • Give leadership a clear view of sales and demand trends over time
  • Identify stores, regions and categories driving growth, volatility and stock risk
  • Support operational planning around replenishment and promotions
  • Explore how external factors align with changes in demand and sales

dashboard modules and questions

module 1: commercial overview

  • Views: total sales trend, demand trend, rolling average, store and region comparison, holiday highlighting
  • Questions: Are sales growing or flattening? Which stores dominate revenue? Do holiday periods create clear uplifts?

module 2: stock and demand management

  • Views: inventory level, units sold, units ordered, forecast vs actual, stock risk flags
  • Questions: Where are stockouts likely? Which stores are over-ordering? Where does forecast error appear highest?

module 3: pricing and promotion analysis

  • Views: price, discount, competitor pricing, sales response by category and region
  • Questions: Do discounts drive incremental sales? Which categories are most price-sensitive? Is pricing aligned with competition?

module 4: external drivers

  • Views: weather, holiday/promotion, seasonality overlays, segmented trend analysis
  • Questions: Which external conditions align with changes in demand? Are some categories more seasonal than others? Do weather and holiday effects differ by region?
Suggested KPI: Total sales and sales growth vs prior period
Suggested KPI: Inventory turnover and stock risk rate
Suggested KPI: Forecast accuracy / error rate
Suggested KPI: Discount uplift or price sensitivity measure

expected deliverables

  • Interactive dashboard with clear filters for store, region, category and date
  • Technical note (1 to 2 pages) describing data sources, derived metrics and limitations
  • Brief findings summary with 3 to 5 actionable operational insights

success criteria

Success will be judged on clarity, interpretability, reproducibility and practical usefulness. Strong submissions will explain what the data suggests, what it does not suggest, and how a manager could use the dashboard to prioritise action.
